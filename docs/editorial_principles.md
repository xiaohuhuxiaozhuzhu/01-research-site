# Editorial Principles

1. Keep the source, research question, method, finding, limitation, and next action adjacent.
2. Label the boundary between source observation and editorial synthesis.
3. Prefer qualified language over certainty when the source is partial or contested.
4. Give every brief a human review owner and a modification date.
5. Preserve uncertainty and actor scope in bilingual copy.
6. Do not publish a synthetic fixture as a real result, user story, or platform metric.
