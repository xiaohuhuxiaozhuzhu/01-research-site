CASE 03 / AI GOVERNANCE

Route political context as an editorial cluster.

AI governance is a research theme connected to the product pillar, not a claim to provide legal or policy advice.

PROBLEM

A general AI news site and a policy database would weaken the focused promise of a research workflow tool.

DECISION

Use AI and politics as a satellite cluster connected to AI tools and workflows for researchers.

TRADE-OFF

The positioning is narrower than a news feed, but the content, navigation, and distribution experiments stay coherent.
