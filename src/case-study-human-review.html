CASE 05 / HUMAN REVIEW

Automate preparation, keep publishing human-owned.

The Agent can prepare evidence and flags, but it cannot silently decide that a public claim is ready.

PROBLEM

A fast pipeline can turn a missing source or unsupported number into public copy.

DECISION

Separate retrieval, extraction, rewrite, quality gate, and distribution; unresolved flags block the final state.

TRADE-OFF

The system cannot promise one-click publishing, but every quality decision remains inspectable and reversible.

NEXT EXPERIMENT

Measure rework time and false-positive review flags after a real annotation round.
