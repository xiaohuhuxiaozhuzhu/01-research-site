CASE 02 / EVIDENCE TRACEABILITY

Make provenance a product field.

Different source states imply different publishing boundaries. The interface should make that difference visible.

PROBLEM

Metadata-only records, public abstracts, and verified full text cannot support the same level of claim specificity.

DECISION

Store source status, retrieval date, source URL, evidence quote, and review owner separately.

TRADE-OFF

A visible caveat can make a draft look less polished, but it prevents false confidence and clarifies ownership.
