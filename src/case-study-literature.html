CASE 01 / LITERATURE REVIEW

Reduce repeated reading without hiding the source.

A product decision exercise for researchers who need a triage answer and a defensible evidence trail.

PROBLEM

A one-line summary is fast but often removes the method and caveat needed to decide whether a paper deserves deeper reading.

DECISION

Make question, method, finding, limitation, source status, and next action first-class fields.

TRADE-OFF

The brief takes more space than a headline, but the reader can verify it without reconstructing the workflow.
