PRODUCT CASE STUDIES / PUBLIC SAMPLE

Five decisions behind the workflow.

These case studies are structured product reasoning exercises. They show the problem, evidence, trade-off, and next experiment without claiming production outcomes.

01

LITERATURE REVIEW

Reduce repeated reading without hiding the source.

Problem: researchers need a quick triage answer but cannot trust a summary that removes the method or caveat.

Decision: structure every brief as question, method, finding, limitation, source status, and next action.

Trade-off: the brief is longer than a one-line summary, but the reader can verify the claim without reconstructing the workflow.

Next experiment: moderated task test with five researchers; compare time to find the source and limitation.

02

EVIDENCE TRACEABILITY

Make provenance a product field.

Problem: metadata-only sources, public abstracts, and verified full text imply different publishing boundaries.

Decision: store source status, retrieval date, source URL, evidence quote, and review owner separately.

Trade-off: a visible status can make a draft look less polished, but it prevents false confidence and clarifies the human handoff.

Next experiment: observe which evidence fields researchers check first during a brief review.

03

AI GOVERNANCE

Route political context as an editorial cluster.

Problem: AI governance and politics are important themes, but a digest product should not pretend to be a legal or policy database.

Decision: use AI and politics as a content satellite connected to the research-tools pillar, with explicit editorial boundaries.

Trade-off: the positioning is narrower than a general AI news site, but it keeps the product promise coherent.

Next experiment: compare question-led governance pages with headline-led pages using manual GEO review.

04

BILINGUAL QUALITY

Review qualifiers, not only translations.

Problem: an English-to-Chinese rewrite can preserve nouns while changing uncertainty, scope, or causality.

Decision: evaluate bilingual parity as a separate rubric dimension and display English/Chinese findings beside limitations.

Trade-off: bilingual review increases editorial effort, but it protects the claim boundary for both audiences.

Next experiment: double-blind review of edge cases with two bilingual annotators.

05

HUMAN REVIEW LOOP

Automate preparation, keep publishing human-owned.

Problem: a fast pipeline can turn a missing source or unsupported number into public copy.

Decision: separate retrieval, extraction, rewrite, quality gate, and distribution; unresolved flags block the final state.

Trade-off: the system cannot promise one-click publishing, but every quality decision remains inspectable.

Next experiment: measure rework time and false-positive review flags after a real annotation round.
