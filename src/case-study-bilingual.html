CASE 04 / BILINGUAL QUALITY

Review qualifiers, not only translations.

A bilingual brief is safe only when meaning, scope, causality, and uncertainty survive the language switch.

PROBLEM

Translation can preserve nouns while changing “may be associated with” into “causes” or removing a limitation.

DECISION

Score bilingual parity separately and display English and Chinese findings beside limitations.

TRADE-OFF

Bilingual review increases editorial effort, but it protects the claim boundary for both audiences.
