METHODOLOGY / V0.1

A short brief is only useful when it stays accountable to its source.

AI Research Digest separates what the paper says, what the editor infers, and what a reader should verify next.

01

Source first

Keep a public DOI, arXiv or publisher link beside every brief. Metadata is never treated as evidence for a claim it cannot support.

02

Claims in view

Extract the research question, method, finding and limitation as separate fields. This makes overreach easier to spot.

03

Quality gate

Before distribution, check citation coverage, unsupported specificity, uncertainty language and the boundary between author finding and editorial synthesis.

04

Human review

The local MVP can prepare a draft, but rubric decisions, edge cases and public publishing remain human-owned.

Evidence status

This site contains public-source sample records and synthetic UI fixtures. Search, traffic, user and retention metrics are intentionally not claimed until they are measured on real platforms.
